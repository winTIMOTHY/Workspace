RC Boat Project

This is the RC boat I built with help from my father. We decided to call it The Arrow. It is powered by an 11.1V battery, which supplies power to a roughly 3900 kV brushless motor. The entire system uses both active and passive water-cooling. The frame or chassis was found at a local thrift store, and we removed and replaced all of the original electronics with much stronger ones.

Technical images and details

Battery: 6000mAh 11.1V LiPo

Esc: FrSky 140A

Control: fs-iA6 paired with the fs-i6 radio

Motor: roughly 3600kVA brushless motor

Cooling: water is pushed through a system of tubes that connects to the motor and the esc by a 12v DC pump and then pushed out the side
